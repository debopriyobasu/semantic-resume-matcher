Evaluate matches using these criteria:

- Skills Alignment: Required skills should match the candidate's demonstrated skills.
- Experience Alignment: Years and depth of experience should fit the role expectations.
- Domain Alignment: Industry, product, or technical domain overlap improves confidence.
- Growth Potential: Adjacent skills can support a potential match when core requirements are present.
- Red Flags: Missing critical skills, location conflicts, or compensation mismatch should lower confidence.
